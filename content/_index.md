I work in philosophy of physics: quantum gravity, holography and string theory,
dualities, black holes, and what they mean for the metaphysics of space, time, emergence,
and laws of nature. When I'm not working on these questions, I like to think about modality, scientific realism, and the philosophy and fundations of quantum mechanics and general relativity.

I am a postdoc at the University of Amsterdam (Institute for Logic,
Language and Computation and Institute of Physics), working with Sebastian De Haro on the JTF project *Emergence of Time from Chaos in Operator Algebras*. My work focuses on the
philosophy of physics, especially dualities, holography and string theory,
(quantum) cosmology, and their metaphysical implications.

I received my PhD in 2023 jointly from the University of Geneva and the
University of Urbino. After that, i was a postdoc at the University of Amsterdam, working with Sebastian De Haro, and at the
University of Geneva, in Baptiste Le Bihan's project *Space, Time, and
Causation in Quantum Gravity*.

My work has been recognised with the Giulio Giorello Prize and the Graduate
Student EPSA Prize.
